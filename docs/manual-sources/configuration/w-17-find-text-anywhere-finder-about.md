# W-17 — Find text anywhere (Find bar) & About

- **Find bar:** press **Ctrl+F** (**⌘F** on macOS), or use **Edit → Find**. A slim bar
  drops in at the top-right of the window and searches only that window — the Presenter,
  Slide Editor, Bible Reader and Settings each have their own. Type to search as you go
  and the counter shows `<current>/<total>`; **Enter** goes to the next match and
  **Shift+Enter** to the previous one; **Aa** toggles case-sensitivity; drag the grip on
  the left to slide the bar sideways off whatever it covers; **Esc** closes it and clears
  the highlights. 📸
- **About:** shows the app version and project links.
